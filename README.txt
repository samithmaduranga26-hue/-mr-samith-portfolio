MR. SAMITH — CREATIVE PORTFOLIO WEBSITE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html (internet connection is needed for the Montserrat font).

HOW TO PUBLISH FREE WITH CLOUDFLARE PAGES
1. Create/sign in to a free Cloudflare account: https://dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up
2. Open https://pages.cloudflare.com/
3. Choose Workers & Pages / Create application / Pages.
4. Choose the direct upload option (the exact label may vary), then upload the extracted website folder or a ZIP if the upload screen accepts it.
5. Deploy. Cloudflare will provide a free *.pages.dev address.

CUSTOMIZE BEFORE PUBLISHING
- Replace the portrait placeholder in the hero with your own photo (edit index.html and CSS as desired).
- Replace each ADD THUMBNAIL placeholder with your real project images and video links.
- Replace YOUR_EMAIL@example.com with your real email address.
- Add your phone, location and social links in the contact section.
- Update the biography to your own words and only list tools/skills you genuinely use.

FILES
index.html  — website content
style.css   — layout, colors, responsive design and animations
script.js   — scroll reveal, menu and progress indicator

The website is static HTML/CSS/JS and has no paid dependencies or backend. A custom domain is optional and would normally cost money; the Cloudflare Pages subdomain is free. Google Fonts is loaded online; if offline, a system sans-serif fallback is used.
